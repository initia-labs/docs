---
title: 'Enabling Autosign'
---

On an Autosign-enabled application

1. Click “Connect Wallet” and select your wallet of choice or use the
   supported social login options.
2. When prompted, click "Enable auto-signing"
3. This will open a permission window with the following information and
   options:
   - Requested by: The name, URL, and logo of the application requesting to
     enable autosign
   - Duration: How long the autosign will be active for
4. Review the information and options, and click "Enable", "Approve", and sign
   with your main wallet.
5. Enjoy uninterrupted onchain use, all transactions within that scope will be
   auto-signed.

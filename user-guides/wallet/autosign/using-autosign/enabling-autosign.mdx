## How to enable Ghost Wallets and Auto-signing (example)

![ghost wallet.png](/images/ghostwallet.png)

Visit an Initia-based application.

1. Click “Connect Wallet” and select your wallet of choice.
1. Click Settings and toggle Auto-signing on. 
2. An "enable auto-signing" window appears.

   \* Duration (10 min / 1h / 1 day / 1 week / until revoked)

   \* Scope (what actions the app can perform)

Approve and confirm with your main wallet signature. Enjoy uninterrupted onchain use, all transactions within that scope will be [auto-signed.](http://auto-signed.How)

## How to Enable Ghost Wallets and Auto-signing for New Users

![image.png](/images/image.png)

Visit an Initia-based application.

1. Click "Connect Wallet" → “Log in to Initia” and choose between email, Google or Twitter (X).
2. A sign in window appears to proceed with socials sign in.
3. An “Approve Connection” window appears with a newly auto-created wallet.
4. A permission window appears:

\* Duration (10 min / 1h / 1 day / 1 week / until revoked)

\* Scope (what actions the app can perform)

Approve and confirm with your main wallet signature.

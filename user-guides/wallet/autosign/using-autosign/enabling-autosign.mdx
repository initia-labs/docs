## How to Enable Autosign (example)

![image.png](/images/image.png)

Visit an Initia-based application (e.g., Civitia).

1. Click “Connect Wallet” → “Enable auto-signing" and select your wallet of choice.
2. An "approve connection" window appears.
3. The "Enable auto-signing" pops up:
   - Duration (10 min / 1h / 1 day / 1 week / until revoked)
   - Scope (what actions the app can perform)

Approve once "enable" with your main wallet signature. Enjoy uninterrupted onchain use, all transactions within that scope will be auto-signed.

## How to Enable Autosign for New Users

![image.png](/images/image.png)

Visit an Initia-based application.

1. Click "Connect Wallet" → “Log in to Initia” and choose between email, Google or Twitter (X).
2. A sign in window appears to proceed with socials sign in.
3. An “Approve Connection” window appears with a newly auto-created wallet.
4. A permission window appears:
   - Duration (10 min / 1h / 1 day / 1 week / until revoked)
   - Scope (what actions the app can perform)

Approve and confirm once with your main wallet signature.

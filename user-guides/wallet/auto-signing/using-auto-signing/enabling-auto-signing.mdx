---
title: 'Enabling Auto-signing'
---

![ghost wallet.png](/images/ghostwallet.png)

On an application that supports auto-signing:

1. Click “Connect Wallet” and select your wallet of choice.
2. Activate auto-signing from the app.
3. This will open a permission window with the following information and
   options:
   - Requested by: The name and logo of the chain requesting to enable
     auto-signing
   - Auto-signing Duration: How long the auto-signing will be active for

Approve and confirm with your main wallet signature. Enjoy uninterrupted onchain
use, all transactions within that scope will be auto-signed.

---
title: "How to Manage and Revoke Access"
description: "You can revoke auto-signing at any time:"
---


- _Open your wallet Settings → Ghost Wallet Sessions._
- _Review all active sessions by app name and domain._
- _Revoke any session instantly._
- _Set or modify fund/spend limits if applicable._

If an unverified or non-whitelisted domain requests access, the wallet will display a warning and deny activation until you manually approve.

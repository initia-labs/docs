Ghost Wallets are Initia’s next evolution in blockchain user experience, designed to make onchain actions feel as smooth as browsing the internet. They function as temporary delegated wallets that auto-sign approved onchain transactions on behalf of a user’s main wallet. Ghost Wallets use session keys, account abstraction, and Privy’s embedded wallet infrastructure to remove repetitive signature pop-ups. Users keep full custody of their primary wallet and private keys while enjoying faster, smoother onchain interactions across all Initia appchains and supported dApps.

Ghost Wallets are temporary delegated wallets that auto-sign approved onchain transactions on behalf of a user’s main wallet. They use session keys, account abstraction, and Privy’s embedded wallet infrastructure to remove repetitive signature pop-ups. Users keep full custody of their primary wallet and private keys while enjoying faster, smoother onchain interactions across all Initia appchains and supported dApps.
